Clear boundaries

Useful without pretending it is magic.

Here is what exists now, what the preview shows, and what the Cloud pilot does not promise.

Is Stoke Cloud live?

No. Stoke Cloud is not deployed yet. The Cloud page describes a proposed private pilot and states its public availability and boundaries. When the waitlist is open, you can request manual review; that is not acceptance.

What is free in the pilot?

The first 10 accepted participants can receive 30 days of Cloud access at no charge. That does not promise free hardware, electricity, or third-party model-provider bills. There is no public checkout, payment collection, or card processing.

Does the preview show production data?

No. The video and UI stills use sample data to show the product shape. They are not a live Cloud account, customer evidence, or a claim that Cloud is deployed.

Can I use the product today?

Yes: Stoke itself is self-hosted and open source. Start with the repository and the public guides. Cloud access is a separate, not-yet-deployed pilot.
