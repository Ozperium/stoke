# Approved public brand presentation

- Use the three irregular straight-axis log paths in `landing/stoke-mark.svg`; the same base is the favicon. The `stoke` wordmark has no trailing period. The shared header/footer and guide generator use the same asset.
- Home adds a static supported-provider row: OpenAI API, Anthropic API, Ollama and other OpenAI-compatible endpoints. Marks identify supported destination types, not sponsorship or universal native-app compatibility. Vendor sources and MIT catalog notice are in `landing/brands/`.
- Home alone uses a continuous static graphite/silver and coral atmospheric background from navigation through the hero and demonstration. Keep cards opaque and foreground content unchanged. No floating numerals or animated background.
- The illustrative packet has an ember head and fading wake on its one existing permitted route: grey ingress, coral dispatch, charcoal return. It replaces rather than duplicates the original dot. Refusals never dispatch; failed connections have no moving packet; only the permitted retry activates. This is not live telemetry, token usage, spend or measured energy.
- The existing home and public Cloud illustrations use one log-inspired decision-panel surface with level, unclipped content and four stable cardinal attachment points. Do not substitute the whole crossed-log mark for a content container.
- Preserve the approved product model and renderer; privacy, budget refusal and permitted local fallback remain separate illustrative scenarios. No backend, entitlement, auth or provisioning claims are added by the artwork.
- Stop motion on pause, hidden pages, offscreen sections and reduced-motion; retain the static product explanation and gradient. Both motion controls share the illustration clock. Expose the secondary control and hide the original dot only after successful enhancement, resume after page-cache restoration, and retain the original packet if enhancement cannot load.
- All styles/modules are self-hosted; keep strict CSP and `style-src-attr 'none'`. Avoid runtime layout reads inside the animation tick. The static SVG social source and PNG use the same light palette and approved mark.

Verify with `python3 scripts/check_landing.py`, `python3 scripts/test_landing_structure.py`, `node --test scripts/test_burning_numbers.mjs scripts/test_ember_flow.mjs`, and opt-in product browser tests. Browser acceptance covers logo assets, log-outline content/ports, responsive scenarios, true ambience pause/resume, foreground pixel exclusions, reduced motion, hidden/offscreen states, history restoration, strict CSP and no-JS content.
